📁 How Images Work on Your Live Site

Your website now uses the images/ folder for project images, not localStorage. This means:

  • ✅ Images are stored in the images/ folder on your computer
  • ✅ No storage limits (unlike localStorage)
  • ✅ Images load faster on your live site
  • ✅ You can organize images with normal file management

📤 How to Add Images

To add images for your projects:

  1. Open your My Portfolio/images/ folder on your computer
  2. Drag and drop your images directly into that folder
  3. Use the Project Manager to add the filename to your project

Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, SVG

Tip: Use descriptive filenames like telematic-systems.jpg (no spaces)
